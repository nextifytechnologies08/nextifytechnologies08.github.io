How we work

Four stages, one accountable team

No handoffs between agencies. The engineers who scope your project are the ones who ship it.

01

Discover

Workshops to pin down scope, users and success metrics before a line of code is written.

02

Design

Wireframes and a clickable prototype you can test with real users before we build.

03

Build

Two-week sprints, a shared staging environment, and a demo at the end of every sprint.

04

Launch & grow

Deployment, monitoring and an optional retainer for ongoing features and support.

1. Overview

Nextify Technologies ("Nextify", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy describes what personal information we collect through https://www.nextifytechnologies.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to visitors of the Site and to prospective clients and candidates who submit information through our forms.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide directly — your name, email address, phone number, company, and message content when you submit the contact form, careers application form, or email us directly.
  • Resume & application details — when you apply for a role, any details or links you include in your application (such as a resume or portfolio link).
  • Automatically collected information — standard server log data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and referring URL, collected for security and basic analytics purposes.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data (such as financial account details or government ID numbers) through this Site's forms, and you should not include such information in free-text fields.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the contact form.
  • To evaluate and respond to job applications submitted through the careers form.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the Site.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect against fraudulent or unauthorised activity.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use contact-form or application data for unrelated marketing without your separate consent.

4. Cookies

This Site uses minimal, functional cookies/local storage needed to remember basic interface preferences (such as a dismissed banner). We do not currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. If that changes, this policy will be updated accordingly and, where required by law, consent will be requested first.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Site or process submissions (such as our email and hosting providers), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal authorities, where required to comply with applicable law, regulation or valid legal process.
  • A successor entity, in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of business assets, subject to this policy continuing to apply to previously collected data.

6. Data retention

Contact-form enquiries are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain a business record, typically no longer than 24 months unless a client relationship continues. Job applications are retained for up to 12 months for future-role consideration unless you ask us to delete your data sooner.

7. Security

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data collected, including restricted access to submitted form data and secure transmission (HTTPS) of data between your browser and our servers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights & choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children's privacy

This Site and our Services are intended for businesses and professional use and are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to exercise your data rights, contact us at hello@nextifytechnologies.com, or write to us at the address on our Contact page.
